Avoid activating the 16 ambiguous name-collision boards.

Eight tested public job-search endpoints returned valid first pages with an aggregate advertised total of 10,191 jobs. This is an upper-bound sum of advertised board totals, not verified complete, unique, U.S.-only, or Fortune 500-only jobs; boards can overlap. Two totals are exactly 2,000 and can reflect endpoint limits; paginating and independently checking completeness is required. A listing result is not necessarily a complete job description: the collector must fetch detail pages for skills and compensation. Workday display timestamps may be relative; do not claim these are exact publication dates without validating their semantics.
