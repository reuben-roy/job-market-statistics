How to read it

  • Postings are a sample of selected employer boards, not the whole job market.
  • A mention is an estimate, not proof that a skill is required. Skills are matched only in recognized role, responsibility and qualification sections.
  • Role is estimated from the job title, so titles we can’t place are grouped as Other roles.
  • Date filters use the date the job board supplied. Postings with no reliable date are left out when a date filter is set.
  • Adding boards changes the sample, so compare shares rather than raw counts over time.

This is not a measure of all hiring or of your eligibility.
